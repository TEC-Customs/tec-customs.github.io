The Best Software for HVAC & Plumbing Small Businesses (2026)

Disclosure: TEC Customs may earn a commission if you sign up for a product through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. Jobber and Housecall Pro are for businesses in the US and Canada. Full disclosure

By Tim Cathey, TEC Customs — Apple Valley, CA. Prices checked October 16, 2026 on each company's official pricing page. I haven't run an HVAC or plumbing shop on these apps; this guide compares published plans, features, and fees.

HVAC and plumbing shops have needs a generic invoicing app doesn't cover: dispatching techs to emergency calls, flat-rate price books, maintenance agreements that bring customers back every season, financing for a $12,000 system replacement, and tracking equipment at each address. The software that does all of that ranges from about $30 a month to enterprise contracts you have to call sales for.

I compared four platforms that small HVAC and plumbing businesses commonly consider.

1. Jobber — best for owner-operators and small crews

Jobber is easy to learn and priced well for a one-truck shop. Official prices for one user (Jobber pricing):

  • Core – $29/mo billed annually ($39/mo on a 1-year monthly commitment, $49/mo no commitment): online booking, quotes, invoicing with online payments, a simple website, and reports.
  • Connect – $99/mo annually ($139 no commitment): automated reminders, automatic payments, checklists, automated quote and invoice follow-ups, QuickBooks Online sync, and time and expense tracking.
  • Grow – $149/mo annually ($199 no commitment): job costing, optional line items for good/better/best quotes, two-way texting, and custom automations.

Extra users are $29/mo each. Payments are 2.9% + 30¢ by card online, 2.7% + 30¢ with Tap to Pay, and 1% by ACH. Jobber also lists a Home Depot integration for quoting with current product prices. The trial is 14 days on Grow, no card required.

Watch out for: Jobber's plan list doesn't include a flat-rate price book or dedicated maintenance-agreement management the way trade-focused tools do. Annual prepay is non-refundable per Jobber's FAQ.

2. Housecall Pro — best all-around for growing HVAC and plumbing shops

Housecall Pro names HVAC and plumbing first on its list of target trades. The Basic plan is $59/mo billed annually (Housecall Pro pricing) and includes online booking, scheduling and dispatch, estimates and invoicing, payments and consumer financing, a price book, job costing, and review management.

  • Essentials (5 users included): adds routes, flat-rate pricing, checklist automations, photo reports, employee GPS tracking, commissions, and QuickBooks Online sync.
  • MAX (8 users included, $35/mo per extra user): adds a sales proposal tool, Recurring Service Plans (maintenance agreements), route optimization, open API, a dedicated onboarding specialist, and escalated phone support.

The pricing page didn't display Essentials and MAX prices when I checked, so I couldn't verify those prices. Card processing is "as low as 2.59%," bank payments are 1%, and check deposits through the app have no fee. The trial is 14 days on MAX, no card needed, and there's no long-term contract.

Watch out for: The features HVAC shops care most about (flat rate on Essentials, service plans on MAX) aren't on Basic. Get the Essentials/MAX quote in writing.

3. Workiz — worth a look if phones are your bottleneck

Workiz's plans (Workiz pricing) are Standard (scheduling, jobs, estimates, invoices, online payments, price book, QuickBooks, 5 automations), Pro (performance pay, custom reports, AI lead and scheduling tools, 10 automations), and Ultimate (service plans, flat rate, inventory, equipment tracking, sales proposals, purchase orders, multi-day jobs, 30 automations). Its standout is an integrated phone system with call tracking and recording, plus an AI answering add-on.

Prices: The official pricing page did not show dollar amounts when I checked, so I couldn't verify Workiz pricing. Workiz says annual billing saves "$400+" and offers a 7-day free trial with no card required. The phone, answering, and marketing tools are sold separately.

Watch out for: For HVAC, the important features (service plans, flat rate, equipment tracking) are on the top Ultimate plan.

Visit Workiz

4. ServiceTitan — the big-shop option

ServiceTitan is the heavyweight in HVAC and plumbing software, with dispatch boards, call recording, marketing attribution, and detailed reporting. Its pricing page (ServiceTitan pricing) doesn't publish any prices; you have to book a demo for a quote.

Watch out for: No public pricing usually means you'll be negotiating a contract. For a 1–5 person shop, it's typically more system than you need; it makes more sense once you have dedicated dispatchers and office staff.

Visit ServiceTitan

How to choose

  1. One truck, mostly service calls: Jobber Core or Connect, or Housecall Pro Basic. Both have free trials with no card.
  2. You sell maintenance agreements: Housecall Pro MAX (Recurring Service Plans) or Workiz Ultimate. Recurring plans are the steady income that gets HVAC shops through the slow season.
  3. You sell system replacements: Make sure consumer financing and good/better/best proposals are included (Housecall Pro lists both).
  4. Missed calls cost you jobs: Compare Workiz's phone system and the AI answering add-ons from Housecall Pro and Jobber.
  5. Ten-plus techs and an office team: Get a ServiceTitan demo and compare it with the top Housecall Pro and Jobber tiers.
  6. Watch processing fees: On a $9,000 install paid by card, 2.9% is $261. ACH at 1% is $90.

My take

  • Best for solo and small crews: Jobber.
  • Best all-around for HVAC and plumbing: Housecall Pro.
  • Best if phones are the problem: Workiz (once you get a price).
  • Best for large shops: ServiceTitan.
