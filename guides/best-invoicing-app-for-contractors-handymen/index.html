The Best Invoicing Apps for Contractors & Handymen (2026)

Disclosure: TEC Customs may earn a commission if you sign up for a product through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. Jobber and Housecall Pro are for businesses in the US and Canada. Full disclosure

By Tim Cathey, TEC Customs — Apple Valley, CA. Prices checked October 14, 2026 on each company's official pricing page.

If you're a handyman or contractor, you didn't get into the trade to do paperwork. But the job isn't done until you've been paid, and chasing checks eats into your evenings. A good invoicing app lets you write an estimate in the driveway, turn it into an invoice when the job's done, and get paid by card or bank transfer before you're back in the truck.

I looked at five popular options, from simple $7-a-month invoice apps to full field-service platforms. All prices come from the official pricing pages.

1. Invoice Simple — best for "I just need to send an invoice"

Invoice Simple does what it says. Plans on the official page (Invoice Simple pricing):

  • Essentials – $6.99/mo ($69.99/yr): up to 3 invoices per month, online payments, autofill, and read receipts.
  • Plus – $14.99/mo ($149.99/yr): up to 10 invoices per month, photos, your signature, reports, and due-date reminders.
  • Premium – $21.99/mo ($219.99/yr): unlimited invoices, client signatures, premium templates, and deposits.

There's a 14-day free trial of Premium on mobile.

Watch out for: The invoice caps are tight. A busy handyman will blow past 10 a month, so you'll likely need Premium. The pricing page doesn't list processing fees, so I couldn't verify their payment rates. Invoice Simple is invoicing only, with no scheduling.

Try Invoice Simple

2. Joist — best estimates-to-invoices for solo contractors

Joist is built specifically for contractors, and estimates are its strength. Prices from the official page (Joist pricing), month to month:

  • Basics – $12/mo (about $8.34/mo billed annually): up to 5 documents per month, online payments, estimates to invoices, AI tools, and homeowner financing.
  • Pro – $17/mo (about $14.17/mo annually): unlimited documents and clients, your logo, photos, and work orders.
  • Elite – $32/mo (about $26.67/mo annually): license and insurance on documents, change orders, reports, and reviews.
  • Run – $70/mo intro price, normally $100 (30% off at time of writing): adds jobs and visit scheduling.

All plans have a 14-day free trial, no card required. Joist notes its free plan is no longer available.

Watch out for: Basics' 5-document limit includes estimates and invoices, so it runs out fast. Processing fees aren't on the pricing page. Not verified.

Try Joist

3. Square Invoices — best free option

If you want to spend $0 a month, Square is the answer. On the Square Free plan you can send unlimited invoices and recurring invoices, request deposits, set automatic payment reminders, and send estimates and contracts with e-signature (Square pricing comparison).

Fees on Free are 3.3% + 30¢ for card payments on an invoice and 1% (min $1) for ACH bank transfers. Paid plans (Plus at $49/mo, Premium at $149/mo) drop invoice card fees to 2.9% + 30¢ and cap ACH at $10. They also add batch invoices, milestone payment schedules, and auto-converting estimates to invoices.

Watch out for: On a $5,000 kitchen job paid by card, 3.3% is $165. Push customers to ACH (about $10 max on paid plans, 1% on Free) for big jobs. Square isn't a scheduling or dispatch tool for trades.

Try Square Invoices

4. Jobber — best for handymen growing into a real business

Jobber is a field-service platform that handles quotes, scheduling, invoicing, payments, and a client portal. Official prices for one user (Jobber pricing):

  • Core – $29/mo billed annually ($39/mo on a 1-year monthly commitment, $49/mo no commitment): online booking, quotes, invoices with online payments, a simple website, and reports.
  • Connect – $99/mo annually ($139 no commitment): automated reminders, automatic payments, automated quote and invoice follow-ups, QuickBooks Online sync, and time and expense tracking.
  • Grow – $149/mo annually ($199 no commitment): job costing, optional line items for upsells, two-way SMS, and custom automations.

Extra users are $29/mo each. Payments are 2.9% + 30¢ by card online, 2.7% + 30¢ with Tap to Pay, and 1% by ACH. Instant payouts cost an extra 1%. The free trial is 14 days on the Grow plan, with no card required.

Watch out for: The annual price is the lowest, but you pay up front, and Jobber says that payment is non-refundable if you cancel. The cheap Core plan lacks automatic invoice follow-ups, which is one of the best reasons to buy software like this.

Try Jobber

5. Housecall Pro — best for home-service teams

Housecall Pro is Jobber's main rival and is popular with HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and handyman crews. The Basic plan is $59/mo billed annually (Housecall Pro pricing) and includes online booking, scheduling and dispatch, estimates and invoicing, payments and consumer financing, a price book, job costing, and review management.

Essentials (5 users included) adds routes, flat-rate pricing, checklists, GPS tracking, commissions, and QuickBooks sync. MAX (8 users included, $35/mo per extra user) adds sales proposals, recurring service plans, and route optimization. The official page didn't show Essentials and MAX prices in the version I could load, so I couldn't verify those two prices. Housecall Pro says card processing is "as low as 2.59%" and bank payments are 1%. The trial is 14 days on MAX, no card needed, and there's no long-term contract.

Watch out for: At $59/mo, it's overkill if you just need invoices. It's worth it once you're dispatching other people.

Try Housecall Pro

How to choose

  1. Count your invoices. Under 5 a month: Joist Basics or Invoice Simple Essentials. Steady work: Joist Pro ($17) or Invoice Simple Premium ($21.99). Want $0: Square Free.
  2. Do you need scheduling? If you're booking jobs, sending "on my way" texts, and managing a helper or two, Jobber or Housecall Pro will save more time than they cost.
  3. Look at payment fees, not just the subscription. On big jobs, ACH (around 1%) beats cards (about 2.6–3.3%) by a lot.
  4. Annual vs monthly. Annual is cheaper, but you're locked in. Try the free trial first.

My take

  • Cheapest that works: Square Invoices (free).
  • Best simple app for solo contractors: Joist Pro.
  • Best invoice-only app: Invoice Simple Premium.
  • Best for growing into a crew: Jobber.
  • Best for a home-service team: Housecall Pro.
