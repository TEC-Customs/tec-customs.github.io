The Best Appointment Apps for Hair Salons (2026)

Disclosure: TEC Customs may earn a commission if you sign up for a product through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. Jobber and Housecall Pro are for businesses in the US and Canada. Full disclosure

By Tim Cathey, TEC Customs — Apple Valley, CA. Prices checked October 14, 2026 on each company's official pricing page.

Hair salons have scheduling problems that a basic calendar can't handle. A color client needs processing time, so the stylist can fit a blowout in the gap. A balayage might need two stylists. Booth renters want their own payouts. And no-shows on a three-hour color appointment hurt.

The best salon apps handle all of that: processing times, multi-stylist bookings, deposits, and reminders. Here are five strong options, from free to premium, with prices pulled from each company's official site.

1. Vagaro — best overall for most hair salons

Vagaro has been a salon staple for years. It covers booking, POS, inventory, marketing, payroll and rent collection, forms, a branded app, and a custom "MySite" website. On the official page, the 1-person price showed $30.00/month, with $23.99/month for the first 6 months (Vagaro pricing). Vagaro's FAQ says each bookable calendar is $10/month, up to seven, and more employees after that cost nothing extra. That makes Vagaro relatively affordable for a 5–10 chair salon.

You get a 30-day free trial, free data transfer (they say it usually takes 1–2 business days), and a free card reader with Vagaro Merchant Services. Vagaro also supports Buy Now, Pay Later through Affirm, booking through Google, Instagram, and Facebook, and an AI assistant called "Vera."

Watch out for: Vagaro is à la carte, so some features are paid add-ons. Card processing rates aren't on the pricing page, so I couldn't verify them. Ask for a written quote.

Try Vagaro

2. GlossGenius — best for solo stylists and small studios

GlossGenius is loved by independent stylists for its clean booking website and easy app. Official prices (GlossGenius pricing):

  • Standard – $24/mo annually ($28 monthly): custom booking site, unlimited client notifications, email and text marketing, inventory, and packages and memberships.
  • Gold – $48/mo annually ($56 monthly): for teams up to 9. Adds forms and waivers, Google booking and reviews, an automated waitlist, time tracking, and room/resource management.
  • Platinum – $148/mo annually ($168 monthly): for teams of 10+. Adds custom commissions and team analytics.

GlossGenius's comparison table lists processing time, gap time, deposits, card-on-file policies, and multi-provider booking, all features hair salons need. Free same-day payouts are available Monday–Thursday. Instant payouts cost 1.8%. Payroll is an add-on at $40/mo + $6/seat.

Watch out for: No free plan. The in-person card rate on the pricing page was a blank placeholder when I checked, so it's unverified. Online invoicing is 2.9% + $0.30.

Try GlossGenius

3. Boulevard — best for high-end, busy salons

Boulevard is the premium pick. It's known for "Precision Scheduling" and a polished client experience. Official prices (Boulevard pricing):

  • Essentials – $159/mo monthly ($143/mo annual): 1 location, up to 5 providers, 250 free texts, and 1,000 free email blasts.
  • Premier – $260/mo per location monthly ($234 annual), a fall promo price (normally $325/$293): unlimited providers and multi-location.
  • Prestige – $364/mo per location monthly ($328 annual), also a promo price (normally $455/$410): adds forms plus more texts and emails.

All salon plans include processing and finishing times, multi-staff appointments, waitlists, deposits, walk-in check-in, memberships, and commission tracking. Card processing starts at 2.65%, or as low as 1% with "Boulevard Offset," which passes a 3% fee on to clients. Automated campaigns cost $2 per completed appointment, and QuickBooks sync is $45/mo.

Watch out for: It's expensive for a small shop, and you start with a demo, not a self-serve trial. Note that the Premier and Prestige prices above are limited-time promo pricing.

Try Boulevard

4. Fresha — best for budget salons

Fresha costs $19.95/month for one person or $14.95/month per bookable team member (Fresha pricing). Its feature list includes processing and finishing times, group bookings, resources, patch tests (handy for color), memberships, and a POS. The Team plan adds commissions, wages, pay runs, and timesheets. In-person processing is 2.29% + $0.20.

Watch out for: New clients from the Fresha marketplace cost a one-time 20% commission (minimum $6). Your own booking links and returning clients are free. The free trial is only 7 days.

Try Fresha

5. Square Appointments — best free start

The Square Free plan ($0/mo) includes processing time for color services, an online booking site, reminders, and unlimited staff calendars (Square Appointments pricing). Plus ($49/mo per location) adds multi-staff booking, cancellation and no-show fees, and a waitlist. Premium ($149/mo) adds resource management (chairs, bowls, rooms), service cost tracking for back-bar, and team performance reports. Processing is 2.6% + 15¢ in person on Free, dropping to 2.4% + 15¢ on Premium.

Watch out for: No-show fees aren't on Free, and for long color appointments you'll want them.

Try Square Appointments

How to choose

  • Solo stylist: GlossGenius Standard ($24/mo annually) or Fresha Independent ($19.95). Square Free costs $0 if you can live without no-show fees.
  • 3–10 stylists: Vagaro's $10-per-calendar pricing is hard to beat. GlossGenius Gold ($48/mo annually) is a flat price for up to 9 people.
  • Upscale salon, lots of color and memberships: Boulevard, if the budget allows.
  • Booth-rental salon: Vagaro advertises rent collection, and GlossGenius lists separate bank accounts for booth renters. Confirm the details for your setup on a demo.

My take

  • Best overall: Vagaro.
  • Best for solo stylists: GlossGenius.
  • Best premium: Boulevard.
  • Best budget: Fresha.
  • Best free start: Square Appointments.
