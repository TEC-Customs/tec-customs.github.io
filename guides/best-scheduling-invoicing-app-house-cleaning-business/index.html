The Best Scheduling & Invoicing Apps for House Cleaning Businesses (2026)

Disclosure: TEC Customs may earn a commission if you sign up for a product through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. Jobber and Housecall Pro are for businesses in the US and Canada. Full disclosure

By Tim Cathey, TEC Customs — Apple Valley, CA. Prices checked October 16, 2026 on each company's official pricing page. I haven't run a cleaning business on these apps; this guide compares published plans, limits, and fees.

A house cleaning business lives and dies by its calendar. Most of your income is recurring visits (weekly, every two weeks, monthly), your cleaners are spread across town, and you need to get paid without chasing every client after every clean. The right app handles recurring scheduling, reminders, and getting paid automatically, without costing more than one clean a month.

I compared four options that come up most often for residential cleaners: one built only for maid services, two general home-service platforms, and a free option.

1. ZenMaid — best if you only do cleaning

ZenMaid is built specifically for maid services, and it's priced like it. Plans from the official page (ZenMaid pricing), SMS charges not included:

  • Starter – $19/mo: up to 40 appointments per month, calendar, dispatch and map views, limited automated texts and emails to cleaners and customers, a mobile app (without cleaner GPS tracking), and online payments through Stripe and Square plus invoicing. Chat-only support.
  • Pro – $39/mo: unlimited appointments, digital checklists, cleaner GPS tracking, booking forms for your website (with ZenMaid branding), reports, payroll, a "Spotfinder" for fitting new recurring clients into the schedule, and QuickBooks integration.
  • Pro Max – $49/mo: adds cleaner availability and PTO tracking, customer service ratings, all message templates, Mailchimp and Zapier, booking forms with your own branding, and priority support.

All plans have a 14-day free trial, are month to month, and ZenMaid says it will import your contacts and calendar for free.

Watch out for: 40 appointments a month is only about 10 weekly clients, so a real business will need Pro. Text messages cost extra, and payment processing goes through Stripe or Square, so you pay their fees on top. It's not built for quotes on big one-off jobs the way Jobber is.

2. Square — best free option for a solo cleaner

If you're just starting out, Square lets you send unlimited invoices and recurring invoices on the Square Free plan, with automatic payment reminders and deposits (Square pricing comparison). Fees on Free are 3.3% + 30¢ for card payments on an invoice and 1% (min $1) for ACH bank transfers. Paid plans (Plus at $49/mo, Premium at $149/mo) drop invoice card fees to 2.9% + 30¢.

Watch out for: Square Appointments is designed around clients coming to a location or booking a time slot, not dispatching cleaners to homes, so you won't get route maps or cleaner checklists. Fine for one person, clumsy once you have a team. (Square pricing last checked October 14, 2026.)

Visit Square

3. Jobber — best for cleaners who quote, schedule, and bill in one place

Jobber is a general field-service app, and cleaning is one of its big markets. Official prices for one user (Jobber pricing):

  • Core – $29/mo billed annually ($39/mo on a 1-year monthly commitment, $49/mo no commitment): online booking, quotes, invoices with online payments, a simple website, and reports.
  • Connect – $99/mo annually ($139 no commitment): automated client reminders, automatic payments (charge a saved card for recurring work), checklists, automated quote and invoice follow-ups, QuickBooks Online sync, and time tracking.
  • Grow – $149/mo annually ($199 no commitment): job costing, optional add-ons on quotes, two-way texting, and custom automations.

Extra users are $29/mo each (team plans with 5+ users are listed separately on the page). Payments are 2.9% + 30¢ by card online, 2.7% + 30¢ with Tap to Pay, and 1% by ACH. The free trial is 14 days on Grow, no card required.

Watch out for: For recurring cleaning clients, the features you really want (automatic payments and reminders) start on Connect at $99/mo, not Core. Annual plans are paid up front and Jobber says that payment is non-refundable if you cancel.

Visit Jobber

4. Housecall Pro — best for larger cleaning teams

Housecall Pro lists cleaning among the businesses it's built for. The Basic plan is $59/mo billed annually (Housecall Pro pricing) and includes online booking, scheduling and dispatch, estimates and invoicing, payments and consumer financing, a price book, job costing, and review management.

Essentials (5 users included) adds routes, checklists, photo reports, employee GPS tracking, commissions, and QuickBooks sync. MAX (8 users included, $35/mo per extra user) adds recurring service plans, route optimization, and a dedicated onboarding specialist. The pricing page didn't show Essentials and MAX prices when I checked, so I couldn't verify those prices. Card processing is "as low as 2.59%" and bank payments are 1%. The trial is 14 days on MAX, no card needed, and there's no long-term contract.

Watch out for: It's priced for teams. A solo cleaner will pay for a lot they don't use.

Visit Housecall Pro

How to choose

  1. Solo and just starting? Square Free costs nothing until you get paid.
  2. Cleaning only, small team? ZenMaid Pro at $39/mo is the cheapest app here with unlimited appointments, checklists, and cleaner GPS. Add up the SMS and Stripe/Square fees before you decide.
  3. You also do move-outs, deep cleans, or commercial jobs that need quotes? Jobber handles quotes and recurring work well; budget for Connect if you want automatic payments.
  4. Growing crew with dispatch and routes? Compare Housecall Pro and Jobber side by side on their free trials.
  5. Push recurring clients to ACH or autopay. On a $160 biweekly clean, 1% ACH is $1.60 versus about $5 by card.

My take

  • Cheapest that works: Square (free).
  • Best value for a cleaning-only business: ZenMaid Pro.
  • Best all-around for quotes plus recurring work: Jobber.
  • Best for bigger teams: Housecall Pro.
