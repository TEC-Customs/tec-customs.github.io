The Best POS Systems for Barbershops (2026)

By Tim Cathey, TEC Customs — Apple Valley, CA. Prices checked October 14, 2026 on each company's official site.

A barbershop POS has to do more than ring up a fade. It needs to handle walk-ins and appointments, split tips, track chair rent or commissions, and take a card fast so the next client can sit down. Most barbers today want booking and checkout in one app, not two.

I compared five of the most-used options for barbers and shop owners. Every price below comes from the company's own pricing page. Where I couldn't confirm something, I say so.

1. Square — best all-around POS

Square is the name most shop owners already know, and for good reason. Its hardware is everywhere, and the software is simple. With Square Appointments, you get booking and checkout together. The Free plan is $0/month: you pay only processing. Plus is $49/month per location, and Premium is $149/month per location (Square Appointments pricing).

Plus adds things barbershops care about: cancellation and no-show fees, a waitlist, and staff tools like timecards, shift scheduling, and tip pooling and commission tracking. That last part matters if you pay barbers on commission. Square also supports Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android, so no hardware is required to start. Hardware options start at $59 according to Square.

Processing is 2.6% + 15¢ in person on Free, dropping to 2.5% + 15¢ on Plus and 2.4% + 15¢ on Premium. Paid plans have a 30-day free trial.

Watch out for: Square isn't built just for barbers. Booth-rent collection isn't a headline feature the way it is on SQUIRE. Check that it fits how you pay your chairs.

Try Square

2. SQUIRE — best barbershop-specific system

SQUIRE is made only for barbershops, and it shows. Plans on the official page (SQUIRE pricing):

  • Independent – $30/mo: for solo barbers. Online booking, no-show protection, scheduling, automated email/SMS reminders, a "Flex" branded app, and reporting.
  • Pro – $50/mo: for single-location shops. Multiple barber accounts, Google & Instagram booking, shop management and waitlist, a dedicated onboarding specialist, and client transfer.
  • Executive – $150/mo per shop: adds unlimited email & SMS marketing, a Google Reviews booster, and commission & rent collection. Branded landing pages are a $25/mo add-on, and their AI "Operator" is a $99/mo add-on.
  • Titan – $250/mo per shop: multi-location support, a fully branded app, loyalty, gift cards, and inventory.

SQUIRE also supports Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android and says client data isn't sold or shown to other businesses.

Watch out for: The features many shop owners want most, rent and commission collection, start at the $150 Executive tier. SQUIRE's pricing page didn't list card processing rates, so I couldn't verify their processing fees. Ask before you sign.

Try SQUIRE

3. Booksy — best flat-price booking + POS

Booksy is huge with barbers because clients can find you in its app. It costs $29.99/month plus tax, with $20/month per additional user, and Booksy says every feature is included (Booksy pricing). That includes waitlists, no-show protection, gift cards, memberships, and 2,000 free marketing texts per month.

For checkout, Booksy sells its own card readers: the Stripe Reader M2 at $53.10 and the Stripe Reader S710 at $299 (plus shipping). Processing is 2.49% + $0.10 on the Booksy card reader, 2.49% + $0.20 with Tap to Pay, and 2.69% + $0.30 for mobile/keyed payments. Next-business-day payouts are free. 30-minute fast payouts cost 1.5%.

Watch out for: The optional "Boost" marketplace promotion charges a one-time 30% fee on a new client's first visit, max $100. It's free to leave off.

Try Booksy

4. theCut — best for independent barbers on a budget

theCut is a barber-only booking app with a big consumer side. Its official pricing page lists a Free plan at $0/month, a PRO plan at $25/month (the App Store shows $24.99), and a Shop plan for owners at $0/month (theCut pricing). You get a 30-day PRO trial. According to theCut's help center, PRO adds bookability, search visibility, client blasts, and automation (theCut help article).

For payments, theCut's help center says Mobile Pay costs barbers 2.75% per transaction, and clients pay a $0.95 processing fee. Standard deposits are free, and instant deposits cost 1.5%. The same article says barbers who process through Mobile Pay may qualify for free PRO.

Watch out for: theCut is mostly a booking-and-mobile-payment app, not a full counter POS with inventory and receipt printers. Some clients won't like the $0.95 fee. App Store reviews also mention a per-new-client fee for a "growth" feature. I couldn't confirm the amount on the official pricing page, so read their terms before you turn it on.

Try theCut

5. Fresha — lowest monthly cost with a full POS

Fresha includes a point of sale, retail and inventory, gift cards, memberships, and tips in its plans. It costs $19.95/month for one person or $14.95/month per bookable team member (Fresha pricing). In-person processing is 2.29% + $0.20 (+ $0.10 per Tap to Pay authorization), and terminals start at $139. The Team plan adds commissions, wages, pay runs, and timesheets, which helps shops that pay commission.

Watch out for: New clients who find you through the Fresha marketplace cost a one-time 20% commission, minimum $6. Your own link and returning clients are free.

Try Fresha

How to choose a barbershop POS

  • Solo barber, tight budget: theCut (Free or $25 PRO) or Square Free ($0). Fresha Independent ($19.95) has the most built-in POS features for the money.
  • Shop with 3–8 chairs, booth renters: SQUIRE Executive ($150/shop) for rent and commission collection, or Square Plus ($49) if commission tracking is enough.
  • You want marketplace clients: Booksy and theCut have big client-side apps. Just know the fees for marketplace-sourced clients.
  • Lowest card fees: Among the rates I could verify, Booksy's reader (2.49% + $0.10) and Fresha (2.29% + $0.20) are at the low end. Square's rate drops as you move up plans.

Run the numbers on your own monthly card volume. On $8,000 a month in card sales, a 0.3% difference in rate is about $24 a month, which can matter more than the subscription price.

My take

  • Best overall POS: Square. It's flexible, has cheap hardware, and the free plan lets you start.
  • Best built for barbershops: SQUIRE.
  • Best for solo barbers: theCut or Booksy.
  • Best low-cost full POS: Fresha.
