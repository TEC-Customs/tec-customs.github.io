The Best Booking & Scheduling Apps for Nail Salons (2026)

By Tim Cathey, TEC Customs — Apple Valley, CA. Prices checked October 14, 2026 on each company's official pricing page.

If you run a nail salon, you already know the pain: the phone rings while you're mid-fill, someone no-shows on a Saturday, and your appointment book is a mess of texts and DMs. A good booking app fixes most of that. It lets clients book themselves 24/7, sends reminders, takes deposits, and keeps your calendar clean.

The trouble is there are dozens of options and the pricing pages are confusing. So I went through the five most popular choices for nail techs and small salons, pulled the prices straight from their official sites, and wrote down what actually matters for a nail business.

One honest note before we start: I haven't been paid by any of these companies to rank them, and prices change often. Always check the official link before you sign up.

1. Fresha — best for keeping costs low

Fresha is one of the biggest booking platforms in beauty, and its pricing is pretty straightforward. A one-person business pays $19.95 per month on the Independent plan. If you have a team, it's $14.95 per bookable team member per month (Fresha pricing).

Both plans include the calendar, waitlists, point of sale, client records, consultation forms, gift cards, packages, memberships, and a business app. Fresha also gives you 100 free text reminders per month (per team member on the Team plan), then charges $0.02 per text.

What to watch out for: Fresha has a public marketplace where new clients can find you. If a brand-new client books you through that marketplace, Fresha charges a one-time 20% commission (minimum $6) on that first visit. Returning clients are free, and clients who book through your own link, Instagram, or Facebook are free. For many nail techs that's a fair trade for new customers, but you should know it's there.

Card processing is 2.29% + $0.20 in person and 2.79% + $0.20 online.

Good fit if: You want a full-featured system without a big monthly bill.

2. Booksy — best "everything included" pricing

Booksy's pitch is simple: one price, every feature. It costs $29.99 per month plus tax, and $20 per month for each additional user (Booksy pricing).

That price includes unlimited bookings, reminders, email marketing, 2,000 free marketing texts per month, custom forms and waivers, Reserve with Google, waitlists, no-show protection, gift cards, memberships and packages. For a nail salon that wants to run promos by text, that included text allowance is a real plus.

Processing is 2.49% + $0.10 with Booksy's card reader, 2.49% + $0.20 with Tap to Pay, and 2.69% + $0.30 for mobile/keyed payments. Next-day payouts are free.

What to watch out for: Booksy has an optional "Boost" feature that promotes you on its marketplace. If you turn it on, you pay a one-time 30% fee on a new Boost client's first visit, up to $100. It's off unless you turn it on.

Good fit if: You want predictable pricing and lots of built-in marketing.

Try Booksy

3. GlossGenius — best for solo nail techs who care about branding

GlossGenius is popular with independent techs because the booking website it builds for you looks clean and professional. The Standard plan is $24/month billed annually ($28 month to month). Gold is $48/month annually ($56 monthly) and adds forms and waivers, Google booking and reviews, an automatic waitlist, and time tracking (GlossGenius pricing).

Standard already includes a custom booking site, unlimited client notifications, email and text marketing, inventory, and packages/memberships. There's a 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

What to watch out for: When I checked, GlossGenius's pricing page showed its in-person card rate as a blank placeholder, so I could not verify their in-person processing rate. Online invoicing is listed at 2.9% + $0.30. Ask them for the current in-person rate before you commit. GlossGenius also has no free plan.

Good fit if: You're a one-chair or small studio and want your booking page to look like a boutique.

Try GlossGenius

4. Vagaro — best for salons with several techs

Vagaro is a full salon-management system: booking, POS, payroll, inventory, marketing, a branded app, and more. On the official pricing page, the price for one person showed $30.00/month, with an intro rate of $23.99/month for the first 6 months (Vagaro pricing). Vagaro's FAQ says each bookable calendar costs $10/month, up to seven, after which more employees are free. There's a 30-day free trial, and they advertise a free card reader when you sign up for Vagaro Merchant Services.

Vagaro uses an à la carte model, so some features cost extra as add-ons. The pricing page doesn't list card processing rates, so I couldn't verify Vagaro's processing rate from the official page.

Good fit if: You have 3–7 techs and want one system that can grow into payroll and multiple locations.

Try Vagaro

5. Square Appointments — best free starting point

If you're just starting out, Square is hard to beat on cost. The Square Free plan is $0/month: you only pay processing fees. It includes an online booking site, appointment reminders, unlimited staff calendars, and Instagram, Facebook, and Google booking buttons (Square Appointments pricing).

Paid plans are Plus at $49/month per location and Premium at $149/month per location. Plus adds cancellation policies and no-show fees, multi-staff booking, and a waitlist. Both paid plans have a 30-day free trial.

Processing on the Free plan is 2.6% + 15¢ in person and 3.3% + 30¢ online. It drops to 2.5% + 15¢ on Plus and 2.4% + 15¢ on Premium.

What to watch out for: No-show fees and cancellation policies aren't on the Free plan. For a nail salon, losing deposits and no-show protection is a big deal. Many techs start free and move up to Plus once no-shows start costing real money.

Good fit if: You're a new or part-time nail tech and want zero monthly cost.

Try Square Appointments

How to choose

Ask yourself three questions:

  1. How many people need a calendar? For a solo tech, Fresha Independent ($19.95), GlossGenius Standard ($24 annually), or Square Free ($0) keep costs lowest. If you have a team, do the math: Fresha's per-person price and Booksy's $20 per extra user add up differently than Vagaro's $10 per calendar.
  2. How bad are your no-shows? Make sure the plan you pick includes deposits and card-on-file no-show fees. On Square, that means Plus or higher.
  3. Do you want marketplace clients? Fresha and Booksy can send you new clients, but they take a cut of that first visit (Fresha 20%, min $6; Booksy Boost 30%, max $100). If you'd rather fill your book from Instagram and word of mouth, use your own booking link and you won't pay those fees.

My take

  • Tightest budget: Square Free, then move up to Plus when you need no-show protection.
  • Best value for a small team: Fresha.
  • Best all-in-one pricing with lots of texting: Booksy.
  • Best-looking booking page for a solo tech: GlossGenius.
  • Best for a bigger salon that wants payroll and more: Vagaro.

Every app here has a free trial or a free plan. Load in a week of real appointments, send yourself a test booking, and see how it feels on your phone before you move your whole client list.
