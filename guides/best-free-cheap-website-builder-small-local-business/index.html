The Best Free & Cheap Website Builders for Small Local Businesses (2026)

By Tim Cathey, TEC Customs — Apple Valley, CA. Prices checked October 14, 2026 on each company's official pricing page.

If you run a local business, like a barbershop, a taco truck, a handyman service, or a nail salon, your website has a few simple jobs. It should show up on Google, tell people what you do and where you are, show your hours and prices, and make it easy to call, book, or buy. You don't need a $5,000 custom site to do that.

You do need to be careful with "free." Most free plans put the builder's branding on your site and won't let you use your own domain (yourbusiness.com). That's fine for testing, but most real businesses end up on a cheap paid plan. Below are four builders worth your time, with honest pricing from each official site.

1. Wix — best overall for most local businesses

Wix gives you a real free plan: hosting, the drag-and-drop editor, and thousands of templates, no credit card needed (Wix plans). The catch is that the free plan uses a Wix address (username.wixsite.com/sitename) and shows Wix branding. The paid plans remove that.

Paid plans on the official page are shown in USD, billed yearly:

  • Light – about $17/mo: free domain for 1 year, 2 GB storage, and a light marketing suite.
  • Core – about $29/mo: 50 GB, accept payments, basic eCommerce, and scheduling and services (bookings).
  • Business – about $39/mo: 100 GB, standard eCommerce, and more marketing.
  • Business Elite – about $159/mo: for bigger operations.

Every premium plan includes a 14-day money-back guarantee. Wix notes that prices vary by location and the displayed prices are for yearly plans paid up front.

Watch out for: If you need online booking or payments, you need Core or higher. Light won't cut it. The free domain is a voucher for one year, then it renews at the regular price.

Good fit if: You want flexibility and plan to add bookings, a menu, or a small store later.

Try Wix

2. Squarespace — best-looking sites with the least effort

Squarespace templates are known for looking polished out of the box. There's no free plan, but there's a 14-day free trial, no credit card required (Squarespace pricing).

Plans from the official comparison table (annual price / monthly price):

  • Basic – $19/mo annually ($25 monthly): templates, SEO tools, free domain for 1 year on annual plans, 2 contributors, and selling with a 2% transaction fee. Free invoicing on all plans.
  • Core – $29/mo annually ($39 monthly): 0% store transaction fee, a free Google Workspace email for the first year, code injection, an announcement bar, and a mobile info bar.
  • Plus – $49/mo annually ($65 monthly): lower card rates (2.7% + $0.30).
  • Advanced – $99/mo annually ($139 monthly).

Card processing through Squarespace Payments is 2.9% + $0.30 on Basic and Core.

Watch out for: Basic has a 2% fee on online sales on top of card processing. If you sell much online, Core pays for itself. The free domain is only for the first year.

Good fit if: Looks matter, like for a salon, boutique, photographer, or restaurant, and you'd rather not fiddle with design.

Try Squarespace

3. Square Online — best free option if you already use Square

If you already take cards with Square, Square Online is the easiest way to get a free website tied to your POS, item library, and bookings. Square's plan comparison lists a website builder with SEO tools, pickup and local delivery, and social media integration on the Free plan ($0/mo) (Square plan comparison).

In that same table, custom domain connect, custom code and video embeds, and expanded site customization are shown as not included on Free. Reading the table, they appear to require Square Plus ($49/mo per location) or Premium ($149/mo). I'd confirm that with Square before you count on a custom domain with the free plan.

Online card payments on Free are 3.3% + 30¢, dropping to 2.9% + 30¢ on paid plans.

Watch out for: The jump from $0 to $49/mo for a custom domain is steep compared with Wix or Squarespace. If you don't otherwise need Square Plus, a separate builder may be cheaper.

Good fit if: You sell food or retail and want online ordering synced with your Square POS.

4. GoDaddy Website Builder (Airo) — fastest way to get online with AI

GoDaddy's builder is now AI-first. You describe your business and it generates the site: pages, copy, images, even forms and simple web apps. The official page says you can start for free with 50 AI credits a month, no credit card required, and connect your own domain to any published project (GoDaddy Website Builder). GoDaddy says all plans include SEO tools, SSL, forms, analytics, 24/7 phone and chat support, and online store options with GoDaddy Payments.

Watch out for: The page I reviewed didn't list paid plan prices. It says pricing is plan-based with credits that expire each billing cycle and don't roll over. I couldn't verify GoDaddy's paid prices. Check the checkout page and the renewal price before buying.

Good fit if: You want something live this afternoon and are comfortable editing by chatting with AI.

What a local business website actually needs

Whatever builder you pick, make sure your site has:

  1. Your name, address, phone, and hours on every page (footer is fine), matching your Google Business Profile exactly.
  2. A tap-to-call button and a "Book" or "Get a quote" button above the fold on mobile.
  3. Your services and starting prices. People bounce when they can't find a price.
  4. Real photos of your work, your shop, and your team.
  5. Your city in your page titles, for example "Barbershop in Apple Valley, CA."
  6. Reviews or testimonials.

That's 90% of what gets a local business found and contacted.

How to choose

  • $0 and okay with branding: Wix Free or GoDaddy's free start.
  • Already on Square: Square Online Free, but confirm custom domain rules.
  • Cheapest professional setup with your own domain: Wix Light (~$17/mo annually) or Squarespace Basic ($19/mo annually).
  • Need bookings or payments on the site: Wix Core (~$29/mo) or Squarespace Core ($29/mo annually).
  • Want it done in an hour: GoDaddy's AI builder.

My take

  • Best overall: Wix.
  • Best design: Squarespace.
  • Best free for Square users: Square Online.
  • Fastest with AI: GoDaddy.
