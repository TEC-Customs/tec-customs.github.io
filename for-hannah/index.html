Hannah,

I love you. I wanted you to have a little piece of my heart here, something you can come back to whenever you want to feel close to me.

When I think about my future, I see you in it. I picture a home we get to call ours, your things beside mine, and the feeling of knowing that at the end of a long day, we get to come back to each other.

I want the little things with you. Sleepy mornings. Long hugs in the kitchen. Making dinner together, deciding what to watch, and reaching for your hand without even thinking about it. I want all those ordinary moments that become special because we’re sharing them.

You are beautiful to me, Hannah. I want you to feel that when I look at you, when I hold you, and in the way I make room for you in my life. I want you to feel wanted, appreciated, and loved for the person you are.

I know I don’t always find the right words. Sometimes I get quiet or caught up in everything I’m trying to figure out. There is so much I want to learn to say better, and so much love I want to show you in ways you can actually feel.

When I talk about finding better work, having a place of our own, and building a family someday, I’m thinking about the life I want us to have together. I want room for laughter, for rest, for adventures, and for evenings when there’s nowhere else either of us needs to be.

We have so much still ahead of us. I want to keep getting to know you as we grow, keep making memories with you, and keep finding little ways to make you smile. I want years of stories that start with “remember when,” and a home full of things that remind us of a life we built together.

So whenever you come back to this page, imagine me pulling you close and telling you this: I love you. I’m glad I get to love you. And when I picture the days ahead, my favorite part is you.

You, me, and all the ordinary days.
That’s the life I want.

With love,Tim
